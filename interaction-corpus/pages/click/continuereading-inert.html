The gauge that measured nothing

For eleven years the pressure gauge on the number two line reported a steady value. The maintenance log recorded it every morning, and every morning it was the same.

It took a shift supervisor with a spare afternoon to notice that the needle had not moved through two pump replacements and a full shutdown.
