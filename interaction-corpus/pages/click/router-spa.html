click-router-spa

A single-page app with a client-side router. The navigation changes the route's path with history.pushState, and the router renders the view from the URL, also on popstate. The cards and the results are part of the home view only. "Load more" adds ?page=N to the URL with history.pushState and appends that page; the router draws as many pages as the URL says. "Previous page" is disabled (aria-disabled) on page 1, though its handler still sets ?page=0, which the router draws as an empty page. "Sort: newest first" replaces the path with history.replaceState. Click both "Show details" buttons, then "Load more" until there are no more results.
