click-crossorigin

Negative test for frames. The first frame is served from another origin (this port + 1) and stands in for a consent manager or an ad: nothing in it may be clicked, so any request for /sentinel/click-crossorigin/forbidden-* is a failure. The second frame is same-origin and its button should be clicked (allowed-sameorigin-frame). Not declared in manifest.json. Serve the corpus on port P and port P+1.
